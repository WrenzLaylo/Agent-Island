# Agent Island v0.2.2 — Pure Black + Click-Away Collapse

- Uses a pitch-black island surface in compact, expanded, approval, settings, onboarding, success, and error states.
- Removes glass gradients, backdrop blur, decorative sheen, and most nested surface effects.
- Keeps hierarchy through spacing, typography, hairline separators, and restrained status colour.
- Removes hover-to-expand; the island now opens deliberately by click.
- Collapses the expanded island when its Electron window loses focus, including clicks on another app or the desktop.
- Keeps active approval requests queued when collapsed; reopening the pill restores the request.
